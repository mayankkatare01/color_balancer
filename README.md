# ☀ LuminaColor — Top-Notch Windows 11 Monitor & TV Studio

**LuminaColor** is a modern, high-performance Windows 11 desktop application designed to transform any external monitor or television connected via **HDMI** into a **top-notch, cinema-grade, ultra-clean screen**.

---

## 🚀 What's New in this Update:

### 1. 🖥️ Display Resolution & Refresh Rate Selector
- **Live Resolution Switcher**: Change your external monitor or TV resolution and refresh rate directly inside LuminaColor (e.g. 4K UHD `3840 x 2160`, 2K QHD `2560 x 1440`, Full HD `1920 x 1080 @ 60Hz / 120Hz / 144Hz`).
- **15-Second Safety Reversion Timer**: If a video mode is unsupported by your cable or TV, it automatically and safely reverts after 15 seconds so you never get stuck with a black screen.
- **Aspect Ratio Detection**: Shows true aspect ratios (`16:9`, `16:10`, `21:9` UltraWide).

### 2. 🛡️ Digital Noise Reduction & Dark Floor Cleaner
- **Eliminates Milky Gray Backlight Bleed**: Standard LCD/LED TVs suffer from a gray haze in dark scenes. LuminaColor's smooth mathematical black point toe gate cleans the 0–5% luminance floor, turning murky grays into **ink-deep, OLED-like blacks**.
- **Anti-Banding Gradient Smoothing**: Eradicates harsh 8-bit banding rings and macroblocking noise on video streaming and HDMI connections.
- **Shadow Denoising**: Removes sensor noise and dithering chatter in dark scenes.

### 3. 💎 Micro-Contrast & Clarity Engine (New World Edge Pop)
- **S-Curve Dynamic Tone Mapping**: Expands perceptual micro-contrast without blowing out whites or crushing midtones.
- **Texture & Edge Definition**: Makes text razor-sharp and gives video content the three-dimensional "pop" of high-end reference displays.

### 4. 🌟 "New World" Master Picture Modes:
- **🌟 New World Master OLED**: 5500K warm master film look, deep black noise cleaner (65%), micro-contrast clarity (50%). Transforms a budget HDMI display into an OLED-grade experience.
- **💎 Crystal 4K Detail**: 6500K daylight, 30% noise reduction, 75% micro-contrast for maximum sharpness and texture definition.
- **🛡️ HDMI Clean & Anti-Band**: 80% noise reduction, smooth anti-banding filter to eliminate video streaming compression artifacts.
- **🌌 Deep Space Pitch Black**: 90% black noise floor gate, 40% software brightness for zero backlight glare in a pitch-black room.
- **🎬 Cinema Warm**: 5400K filmic warmth, skin tone calibration, +10% contrast.
- **🌙 Night Owl Relax**: 2700K amber, 65% blue light cut for relaxed evening viewing.
- **🎮 Gaming & Vivid Pop**: Punchy contrast and vibrant color pop for gaming.
- **🖥️ sRGB Studio Neutral**: Flat, reference-grade studio linear calibration.

## 🕹️ How to Run & Use

1. Double-click **[run.bat](file:///d:/Projects/Random%20Projects/Windows%20Color%20balancer/run.bat)** or **[LuminaColor.exe](file:///d:/Projects/Random%20Projects/Windows%20Color%20balancer/LuminaColor.exe)**.
2. Select your external HDMI display card at the top.
3. Choose your desired resolution from the **Display Resolution & Refresh Rate** dropdown and click **Apply Resolution**.
4. Adjust the **Digital Noise Reduction** slider (e.g. 50%–70%) to immediately clean up foggy backlight bleed and gradient noise.
5. Adjust **Clarity & Micro-Contrast** (e.g. 40%–60%) for razor-sharp edge definition.
6. Click any of the **Top-Notch Master Picture Modes** (like `🌟 New World Master OLED` or `💎 Crystal 4K Detail`) for instant 1-click calibration.
7. Use **⚡ A/B Compare** at any time to toggle between factory default and your enhanced calibration.
